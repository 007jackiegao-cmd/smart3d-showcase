# Content provenance and publication scope

The wider project was inspected through its provided source archive. Only a newly prepared demonstration, rewritten technical documentation, dependency licenses and actual demo screenshots are public. The original backend and its internal algorithms, runtime files, source archive and database backup remain outside this repository.

The public interface and its focused constructors were prepared with AI assistance. They expose a reproducible illustration of the project's workflow and are not presented as the unchanged historical application. No personal biography, school, employment history, contacts, local user directories, real user records or credentials are included.

Models and material assumptions are synthetic examples. Screenshots depict this demonstration only, without personal photographs or EXIF metadata. Platform account handles and standard commit/hosting metadata remain platform information; this package makes no claim of complete unlinkability to an existing account.

Third-party libraries retain their authors' licenses and attribution. Boolean computation is provided by Manifold. No original algorithm invention, unverified ownership of legacy material or additional license for private source is asserted.
